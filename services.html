Common questions

What is a Certificate of Compliance and when do I need one?

A Certificate of Compliance (CoC) confirms that your electrical installation meets SANS 10142-1. You need one when you sell a property, after new installation work, and often for insurance claims.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

Quotes for planned work are free. Repairs and emergency call-outs have a call-out fee, which we confirm on the phone before we travel to you.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across Polokwane, Seshego, Mankweng, Bendor, Flora Park and nearby areas. See the contact page for the full list.

How quickly can you start?

Most repairs can be booked within one or two working days. Larger jobs are scheduled once you accept the quote.
