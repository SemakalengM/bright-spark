What we do

From a single faulty plug point to wiring a new house, we handle residential and small commercial electrical work of every size.

  • Electrical installations

    Distribution boards, plug points, stoves, geysers and backup power.

  • Repairs and fault finding

    Tripping breakers, power failures, burnt plugs and earth leakage faults.

  • House wiring

    New builds, extensions and full rewiring of older homes.

  • Lighting installation

    Indoor, outdoor, security and energy-saving LED lighting.

  • Maintenance

    Inspections, compliance certificates and planned maintenance.
