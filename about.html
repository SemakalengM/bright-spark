About Bright Spark

A local, family-run electrical contractor that has been keeping Polokwane powered since 2014.

Our story

Bright Spark Electrical Services was started by qualified electrician Thabo Mokoena, who spent eight years working for a large contractor before deciding that homeowners and small businesses deserved the same standard of work at a fair price.

What began as one electrician and a single bakkie has grown into a team of six, including three registered electricians and two apprentices. We still answer our own phones, and most of our new work comes from customer referrals.

Today we complete more than 600 jobs a year, from small repairs to full house wiring, across Polokwane, Seshego, Mankweng and the surrounding areas.

Our mission

To provide safe, reliable and affordable electrical services, completed by qualified people who respect our customers' homes, time and budgets.

Our vision

To be the most trusted electrical contractor in Limpopo, known for honest advice and work that is done right the first time.

Our values

These values guide how we work on every job, large or small.

  • Safety firstEvery installation meets SANS 10142-1. We never cut corners that put people or property at risk.
  • HonestyWe explain the problem in plain language and recommend only the work you actually need.
  • ReliabilityWe arrive when we say we will and finish when we say we will.
  • RespectWe treat your property with care and leave every site clean.
  • GrowthWe train local apprentices and keep our skills current with new technology such as solar and backup power.

Our history

A few milestones from the last twelve years.

  1. 2014Bright Spark is founded in Polokwane with one electrician and one vehicle.
  2. 2017First apprentice joins the team and the business moves into its own workshop.
  3. 2020We add inverter and backup power installations in response to load shedding.
  4. 2023The team grows to six people and a second service vehicle is added.
  5. 2026We launch this website so customers can request quotes online.
